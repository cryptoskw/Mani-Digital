04 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Reviewed September 2026

Should I put keywords in the business name?

Use the real-world business name. Adding unsupported keywords can create policy and trust problems.

Can I create profiles for every suburb I serve?

Not without genuine eligible locations. Service-area businesses should use one accurate profile and define coverage appropriately.

Why does the website matter to the profile?

The website supports relevance, trust and conversion by explaining services, areas, proof and contact information in more depth.
