10 / IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

Clear before
we begin.

What does Mani Digital mean by a digital asset?

A digital asset is an owned online property built around demand, useful content, search visibility and a conversion or monetization path. It can be a lead-generation site, niche media property, directory, comparison site, ecommerce content asset or useful tool.

Do you promise traffic or income?

No. We do not make wild guarantees. We build the infrastructure, search strategy, content architecture, tracking and conversion system that give an asset a serious chance to grow.

Do you still do SEO and web design?

Yes. SEO and custom website development remain core capabilities. The difference is that we now frame them as part of a larger asset-building system.

Can you build for Johannesburg, Cape Town and national search?

Yes. We start with the market where trust and conversion are strongest, then expand into city and national structures when the site has enough depth and proof.
