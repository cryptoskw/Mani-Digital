Keyword research Johannesburg guide

Keyword research that starts with business value.

A Johannesburg keyword research framework for finding the services, locations and buyer questions most likely to produce qualified enquiries.

Browse all SEO guides
01 / THE OUTCOME

Good keyword research does not produce the longest spreadsheet. It identifies a focused market, assigns every valuable intent to the right page and shows what deserves investment first.

Separate commercial, local and research intent

Map one primary purpose to every important page

Prioritise opportunities by value and difficulty

Avoid duplicate pages competing for the same search

02 / THE CONTEXT

Search volume is useful, but it is not the business case

A broad phrase can attract many searches and very few suitable customers. A lower-volume service-and-location phrase may represent a person ready to compare providers today. Research must therefore combine demand with margins, sales fit and the strength of the available offer.

Johannesburg also contains several distinct commercial nodes. Location modifiers should reflect real service coverage and customer behaviour, not become a reason to clone the same page for every suburb.

Best suited to

Johannesburg service businesses

B2B marketing teams

Companies planning a new website

Businesses restructuring an existing SEO campaign

03 / THE WORK

Build the keyword map in seven deliberate steps

Each step narrows the research until the final map reflects pages the business can actually support with expertise and proof.

01

Start with profitable services

List the services the business wants more of, their approximate value, delivery limits and the type of customer most likely to buy them.

02

Collect customer language

Use enquiries, sales calls, Search Console, internal site search and competitor pages to understand how customers describe the problem and desired outcome.

03

Classify intent

Separate searches for learning, comparing, locating and buying. Different intent usually requires different page depth and calls to action.

04

Study the actual results

Review which page types Google currently rewards. A service page, category, guide, directory and map result indicate different expectations.

05

Create topic clusters

Group close variants around one strong page, then identify supporting questions that deserve separate guides rather than duplicating the money page.

06

Score the opportunity

Balance estimated demand, relevance, commercial value, current authority, competition and the effort required to create the best available answer.

07

Assign and sequence URLs

Give each priority intent one page owner, define supporting internal links and build the cluster in an order that strengthens the main commercial opportunity.

05 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions this guide should settle.

Reviewed 15 September 2026

How many keywords should one page target?

One page can rank for many close variations when they share the same intent. The better question is whether a searcher expects the same answer, offer and next step.

Should Johannesburg suburbs each have a page?

Only where the business has genuine relevance and enough distinct information to help customers. A strong Johannesburg page can cover related areas until separate demand and proof justify expansion.

Are low-volume keywords worth targeting?

Yes, when they describe a valuable service or urgent problem. SEO tools estimate demand and may understate specific local searches.

What causes keyword cannibalisation?

Cannibalisation appears when several pages serve the same intent without a clear hierarchy. Improve, merge, redirect or differentiate them according to the best page purpose.
