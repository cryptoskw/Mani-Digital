05 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Reviewed September 2026

Can you do local SEO if our office is not in Cape Town?

We do not create fake Cape Town offices. If your business genuinely serves Cape Town remotely or through a team, we build transparent service-area content and conversion paths around that reality.

Do Cape Town businesses need suburb pages?

Only when the page has a real reason to exist. Useful area pages should explain service coverage, proof and practical customer information, not repeat the same copy with a different suburb.

Is Google Business Profile part of local SEO?

Yes. Profile categories, services, photos, reviews, links and activity can influence local discovery, but they work best when the website supports the same message.
