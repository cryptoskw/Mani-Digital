SEO audit checklist

A business-first SEO audit checklist.

Use this checklist to examine search opportunity, indexation, page purpose, internal links, content, local signals and conversion before buying disconnected fixes.

Browse all SEO guides
01 / THE OUTCOME

A useful SEO audit identifies the few constraints that matter most, proves them with evidence and turns them into an ordered implementation plan.

Check whether important pages can be indexed

Find duplicated or unclear search intent

Review internal links and conversion paths

Turn findings into a prioritised roadmap

02 / THE CONTEXT

An audit is a decision document, not a score

Automated tools are useful for discovery, but they do not know which service is profitable, which location is real or whether an indexed page helps a customer make a decision. A long error export can hide the issues that actually suppress growth.

Begin with the business and the search opportunity. Then check whether the website gives search engines a clean route to distinct, useful pages and gives visitors enough clarity and proof to act.

Best suited to

Owners reviewing an underperforming site

Marketing teams preparing an SEO brief

Developers checking a launch

Businesses comparing audit proposals

03 / THE WORK

The six audit layers that deserve evidence

Work through the layers in this order so technical detail remains connected to commercial value.

01

1. Business and demand

List profitable services, target areas, ideal customers and the queries already producing visibility. Confirm that the opportunity is commercially worth pursuing.

02

2. Crawling and indexing

Check robots controls, XML sitemaps, status codes, canonicals, redirects, noindex directives and rendered content for important URLs.

03

3. Page ownership

Assign one primary purpose to each important URL. Merge, redirect or differentiate pages that compete for the same intent without adding value.

04

4. Content and evidence

Review whether each page answers the buyer's task with accurate detail, expert input, proof, useful media and a clear next step.

05

5. Internal authority

Confirm that navigation, hubs, contextual links and breadcrumbs connect related pages and make priority URLs easy to discover.

06

6. Measurement and action

Validate analytics and Search Console, define the baseline, rank fixes by impact and effort, assign owners and schedule verification.

05 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions this guide should settle.

Reviewed 15 September 2026

Can I do an SEO audit myself?

You can complete a useful first review with Search Console, analytics, a crawler and manual checks. Complex rendering, migrations, international targeting or large catalogues may need specialist diagnosis.

How often should a website be audited?

Audit after major launches or migrations and whenever visibility changes unexpectedly. A focused quarterly review is often more useful than repeating a full generic audit every month.

What should be fixed first?

Prioritise issues that block important pages from being discovered, indexed, understood or converted. Severity should reflect business impact, not only how many URLs a tool reports.

Does an audit include implementation?

Not automatically. Confirm whether the scope covers diagnosis only, developer instructions, content changes, implementation and post-fix verification.
