02 / THE CONTEXT

Good consulting helps a business spend in the right order

Many SEO failures come from doing reasonable tasks at the wrong time. A team may publish articles when core services are weak, rebuild a website without redirects, or chase national rankings before local proof exists.

Consulting gives owners and teams a clear decision layer: what matters, what can wait, what is risky and what success should look like in the next phase.
