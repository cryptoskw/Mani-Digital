02 / THE CONTEXT

There is no honest price without a defined problem

A five-page local service website and a national ecommerce catalogue do not need the same research, technical work, content or authority. Pricing should reflect the starting condition, the value of the target market and the resources required to close the gap.

The lowest quote can become expensive when it produces thin pages, weak links or reports without implementation. The highest quote is not automatically better either. Ask what will be delivered, who will do it, what must come from your team and how priorities will be reviewed.

03 / THE WORK

Six factors that shape an SEO quotation

The quotation should make these variables visible. If the supplier cannot explain them, the number is difficult to evaluate.

01

Current website condition

Indexing problems, an unsafe migration, duplicated templates or an unclear service structure create repair work before growth work can compound.

02

Competition and authority gap

Established results may have years of useful content, links, reviews and brand demand. Closing that gap takes more than changing title tags.

03

Number of markets

One Johannesburg service cluster is a different scope from several cities, industries, languages or a national product catalogue.

04

Content and subject input

Strong pages require research, expert knowledge, examples and approval. Cost changes according to who supplies, writes and reviews those inputs.

05

Implementation responsibility

A strategy-only engagement costs less than research, writing, development, publishing, measurement and ongoing iteration delivered together.

06

Speed and opportunity cost

A compressed launch may require more people at once. A slower roadmap may cost less monthly but delay learning and potential enquiries.

05 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions this guide should settle.

Reviewed 15 September 2026

How much should a small Johannesburg business spend on SEO?

There is no universal amount. Start with the value of a qualified customer, realistic search demand and the work required to create a credible result. A focused project can be better than an underfunded retainer that never reaches implementation.

Is a monthly SEO retainer necessary?

Not always. Audits, migrations and architecture work can be defined projects. Competitive markets often benefit from ongoing content, authority, local work and iteration after the foundation is repaired.

What should an SEO proposal include?

It should state the opportunity, baseline, deliverables, responsibilities, timing, measurement approach, assumptions and review points. Avoid proposals that promise rankings but cannot name the first pages or problems.

When is cheap SEO risky?

Risk rises when the price cannot support research, original content, careful implementation or quality control. Ask exactly how many hours and deliverables the fee can realistically fund.
