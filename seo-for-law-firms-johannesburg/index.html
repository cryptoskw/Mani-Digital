04 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Reviewed September 2026

Should every legal service have its own page?

Only when it represents a distinct client need and the firm can provide meaningful, accurate information. Closely related minor services may be clearer together.

Can SEO attract better-fit legal enquiries?

Focused service pages, qualifying information and clearer consultation expectations can improve relevance, though no channel can eliminate unsuitable enquiries entirely.

Do you write legal advice content?

We structure and draft content from firm-approved subject input. The attorney remains responsible for legal accuracy and final approval.
