02 / THE CONTEXT

The cheapest SEO is expensive when it fixes the wrong problem

A business with broken indexation does not need six months of blog posts as the first move. A site with no convincing service pages does not need reporting dashboards before the core pages exist. A business with no tracking may need measurement before judging a campaign.

That is why our pricing conversation starts with the growth constraint. The engagement can be small and defined, or broader and ongoing, but it must connect to the work that will actually change the outcome.

05 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Reviewed September 2026

How much does SEO cost in Johannesburg?

It depends on competition, website condition, implementation needs and the number of services or locations. We do not force every business into the same package. The first step is to identify the constraint and scope the work honestly.

Do you publish fixed SEO package prices?

Not yet, because the wrong fixed package can waste money. We publish the engagement options and deliverables clearly, then price after reviewing the website and market.

Can we start with a once-off SEO project?

Yes. A once-off audit, technical repair, content architecture project or landing-page build can be the best first move before a monthly campaign.
