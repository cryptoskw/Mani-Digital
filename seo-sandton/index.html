Do we need a physical address in Sandton?

No. We represent the business accurately. Companies with a real customer-facing location can use it; service-area businesses should describe genuine coverage without creating false addresses.

Can one page rank for every service in Sandton?

Usually not when services have different search intent and buying questions. The location page should act as a useful local hub connecting to focused service pages.

How do you avoid thin location pages?

We include distinct market context, genuine coverage, service relevance, local proof and useful decision information rather than changing place names in repeated copy.
