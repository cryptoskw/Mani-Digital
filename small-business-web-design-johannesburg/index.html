How many pages does a small-business website need?

It depends on the services and search demand. A focused five-page site may be enough for a simple offer; businesses with distinct services or locations usually need a broader structure.

Can the website grow later?

Yes. We plan navigation, templates and URLs so new service and content pages can be added without rebuilding the foundation.

Do you supply the wording?

We can write the core copy from your business information, then refine claims and details with you before launch.
