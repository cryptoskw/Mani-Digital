02 / THE CONTEXT

Technical problems often hide behind a normal-looking website

A page can look perfect in a browser while Google receives the wrong canonical, cannot reach its content, discovers thousands of parameter URLs or repeatedly crawls pages with no search value.

We trace symptoms to their source. The deliverable is a prioritised repair plan and implemented corrections where access allows—not a tool export handed back to the client without interpretation.

Best suited to

Sites losing impressions

Website migrations and redesigns

Large catalogues or ecommerce stores

JavaScript-heavy and multi-template websites

03 / THE WORK

The technical layers we diagnose and verify

A crawl is the beginning of the investigation. We connect every finding to an affected page type, search outcome and repair that can be tested.

01

Discovery and crawl paths

Robots controls, navigation, internal links, XML sitemaps and server responses are checked to confirm important URLs can be reached efficiently.

02

Indexation controls

Canonicals, noindex directives, duplicate variants, parameter URLs and rendering are reviewed against the pages the business actually wants in search.

03

Architecture and templates

Service, category, product and guide templates are examined for repeated metadata, weak headings, thin content and unhelpful URL generation.

04

Redirects and migrations

Redirect maps, chains, loops, broken destinations and removed pages are tested before and after redesigns, platform moves or domain changes.

05

Performance and mobile delivery

Large media, blocking resources, layout movement, interaction delay and mobile usability are prioritised according to real page and customer impact.

06

Structured data and validation

Relevant schema, language, canonicals, social metadata, sitemaps and analytics are validated after implementation rather than assumed complete.

05 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Reviewed September 2026

What does a technical SEO audit examine?

We review crawling, index coverage, canonicals, redirects, sitemaps, robots controls, rendering, templates, internal links, structured data, speed and mobile behaviour in the context of the site's goals.

Can you implement the fixes?

Yes, where we have the required access and the platform permits it. Otherwise we provide developer-ready instructions and verify the completed change.

When is a technical audit urgent?

After a migration, sudden impression loss, large content release, domain change, security incident or unexplained indexing problem, technical diagnosis should happen quickly.
