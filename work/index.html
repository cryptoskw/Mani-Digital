HOW WE REPORT PROOF

Proof without oversharing.

Public case studies show selected, rounded third-party estimates. Full dashboards, keyword lists, traffic values and client analytics stay private. During a confidential strategy discussion, we can explain methodology and provide deeper context where appropriate.

For every campaign, we separate leading indicators such as crawling, impressions and ranking movement from business outcomes such as qualified enquiries. That prevents a rising traffic graph from being presented as success when the visitors are not commercially relevant.
